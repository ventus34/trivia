## Limitations

This was a full editorial review with targeted source checks, not a fresh in-game test or a source-by-source verification of every surviving assertion. Some questions still cover related facts and vary in difficulty; the review did not attempt to redesign the entire pack or eliminate all topical overlap. Unchanged records retain their existing verification metadata; modified records do not claim verification by their previous model.
