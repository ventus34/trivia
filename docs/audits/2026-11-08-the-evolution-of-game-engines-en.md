# Audit: The Evolution of Game Engines (English)

Reviewed the description and all 99 questions, options, answer keys and explanations. The description remains unchanged. Corrected or clarified 47 questions, numbered by one-based position:

1, 4, 6, 9, 10, 12, 13, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 27, 29, 31, 35, 39, 41, 43, 46, 47, 55, 59, 61, 62, 67, 69, 70, 73, 74, 75, 79, 80, 81, 82, 84, 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 96, 97, 99.

Corrected Defold's ownership by its independent foundation, the Python origins of Cocos2d, the 1992 release of Comanche, the open-source status of Godot and the use of Unreal in PAYDAY 3. Removed the unsupported PhyreEngine/Gran Turismo assertion and the confusion of Halo Wars with the cancelled Halo MMO Titan. Changed duplicate or overlapping options including Quake/id Tech 2 and MegaTexture/virtual texturing. Distinguished O3DE from a simple Lumberyard rename, clarified Source and HeroEngine history, ScummVM support beyond SCUMM, and current WebXR terminology. Fixed the exact title of Ashes of the Singularity and the mislabeled Crystal Dynamics engine. Modified records no longer retain their previous model verification metadata.

## Limitations

All 99 items received editorial review supplemented by targeted source checks; not every remaining historical attribution was independently verified from original developer documents. Engine names, ownership and licensing change, and individual games may use customized technology or multiple middleware components. Some questions overlap and the audit preserves the original number of records.
