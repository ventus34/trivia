These questions were checked against the sources below. They are historical and educational material rather than individual medical advice. The package still contains 80 questions identical in wording to `medicine_and_human_body_en` (a direct comparison of the question fields); corresponding records 26–105 also match in options, answers and explanations. The remaining overlap requires further editorial work, and this partial replacement is not a new independent medical review of those 80 records. The two original package IDs were retained because selections are stored by ID in the application.
