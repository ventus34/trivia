# Audit: Otaku Culture (English)

Reviewed the description and all 100 questions, choices, answer keys and explanations. The description fits the pack and remains unchanged. Corrected or clarified 49 questions, identified below by one-based position; the pack retains 100 questions.

- **Ambiguous or incorrect keys and clues:** 4, 7, 8, 19, 21, 22, 25, 31, 36, 38, 39, 42, 43, 45, 46, 47, 48, 50, 52, 55, 59, 62, 67, 71, 78, 81, 82, 83, 85, 87, 88, 90, 91, 92, 95, 96, 98. In particular, Cardcaptor Sakura and Sailor Moon appeared in Nakayoshi, not Ribon; senpai/sempai and wotagei/otagei are alternative spellings rather than distinct answers. The anime-synthesis questions now distinguish Yamaha's engine from Crypton's Miku voicebank, and the figure question specifies the relationship between Max Factory and Good Smile. Replaced several questions lacking a discriminating clue and clarified the C3 AFA collaboration and the maid-café greeting versus food chant.
- **Explanations, terminology and factual context:** 1, 29, 32, 49, 51, 58, 69, 75, 76, 77, 86, 89. Removed internal blueprint language, unsupported superlatives and misleading claims. Modified questions no longer retain verification metadata from the previous model.

## Limitations

This was a full editorial review supplemented by targeted source checks, not independent verification of every surviving assertion. Some terminology has variant spellings and changing uses, and company or event details can change. Related topics recur in the pack; this audit did not attempt a full redesign or remove all topical overlap.
