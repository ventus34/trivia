# Audit: Iconic Rides of Pop Culture

Reviewed all 100 question prompts, options, keys and explanations, plus the pack description. Changed 32 records: 3, 6, 8, 11, 13, 16, 22–24, 31, 36, 39, 45, 50, 53, 58, 65, 67, 70–73, 75, 81, 84, 89–91, 93, 95, 98 and 100. Changed entries no longer carry their old verification metadata; question count and description remain unchanged.

Corrections include the early *Paper Moon* car’s year, Bumblebee’s different film forms, the F-250 associated with *True Detective*, and the Gatsby car’s yellow/cream descriptions. Questions with unsupported details, a nonexistent *Seinfeld* episode, overlapping answers, or claims about invented official names were narrowed or replaced. The Ford GT40 explanation now credits multiple participants in its development. Some substitutions use broader, verifiable cultural details instead of unsupported exact specifications.
