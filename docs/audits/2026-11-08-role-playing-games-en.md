Reviewed the description and all 109 questions, options, answer keys and explanations. The description remains appropriate and unchanged. Corrected or clarified 54 questions, numbered by one-based position:

Corrected Dzikie Pola second-edition tests to d20, Final Fantasy XI's first Japanese release to 2002, Ragnarok Online's Korean commercial launch to 2002, and Star Ocean: Till the End of Time to a console action RPG. ## Limitations

All entries received an editorial review, supplemented by targeted source checks, not a replay of each game or primary-source confirmation of every remaining assertion. RPG terminology and mechanics vary by edition, game and group; where relevant the question now identifies an edition. Some topics recur, and the audit preserves all 109 questions rather than undertaking a complete redesign.
