Reviewed the description and all 100 questions, choices, keys and explanations. The existing description fits the category and remains unchanged. Corrected or clarified 73 questions; numbers are their one-based positions in the pack:

Many original prompts had no identifying clue or offered multiple correct answers. Reworded them around specific designations, ships, variants, dates or nicknames; removed alternate names of the same item from distractors. Corrected, among other things, the Ka-52's Russian name versus NATO reporting name, Austrian origin of the Steyr AUG, the Mk 153 SMAW designation, the AUF1/GCT 155 synonym, the AIM-7 origin of the Sea Sparrow, the P-47/A-10 distinction, the 1945 commissioning of USS Midway, and competing U.S. nuclear carrier classes. ## Limitations

The entire pack received an editorial review, with selected claims checked against the sources above; every remaining assertion was not independently checked against primary documentation. Equipment names are reused across ships, variants and operators, and historical specifications or terminology may differ by source. Some factual subjects still recur across questions. The audit did not involve examining military equipment or testing the game.
